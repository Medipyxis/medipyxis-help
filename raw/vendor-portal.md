# Vendor portal

The vendor portal (`/vendor`) is the part of Medipyxis used by companies that supply clinics. Vendor staff sign in on the same Medipyxis sign-in page as clinic staff; a vendor account then opens the vendor portal home page.

## Roles

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Admin (`vendor_admin`) | Every page, plus Staff, API (API keys and webhooks), and each clinic's terms |
| Staff (`vendor_user`) | Every page except Staff, API, and clinic terms |
| Sales (`vendor_sales`) | Same as Staff |

## Sidebar

Home, Catalog, Shipments, Orders, Requests, Verifications, Use Events, Consignment, Supply Radar, Invoices, Clinics, CRM, Messages, Documents. Admins also see Staff and API.
