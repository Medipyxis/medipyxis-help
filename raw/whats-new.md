# What's new, in video

Short silent videos of recent changes in Medipyxis, each with a description of what is on screen and why it matters.

## How do I search the Fax Review Queue?

[Search the fax queue](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/fax-queue-search/) · 1 short video

You can search the Fax Review Queue by patient name or by a sender fax number, and you can widen the search to all fax text so a phrase in the subject is found too.

## How does Use last order fill the MRN?

[Use last order and the MRN](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/use-last-order-mrn/) · 1 short video

Use last order copies the patient’s previous order of that type into a new draft. If that earlier order’s ID / MRN was empty, the new form fills the chart MRN.

## How do I pick a wound from today's visit on an order?

[Today's wounds on an order](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/order-picker-todays-wounds/) · 1 short video

The wound dropdown on an order now includes wounds documented on today’s unsigned visit, labelled “(new from today’s visit),” as well as wounds already on the chart.

## How does a new order form get the chart MRN?

[Chart MRN on a new order](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/order-form-mrn-prefill/) · 1 short video

When you open a new Wound Care Supplies Order, the ID / MRN box is already filled with the chart MRN. The box stays editable.

## How do I record the indication on a debridement pop-up?

[Indication on debridement pop-ups](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/debridement-indication/) · 1 short video

Indication is now the first field on the Sharp debridement and Enzymatic debridement pop-ups. The walkthrough shows the Indication list, the sharp choices Slough, Biofilm and Other with free text, and the enzymatic choices Necrotic / devitalized tissue and Eschar.

## How do I move my practice's patient data into Medipyxis?

[Practice data import](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/practice-data-import/) · 1 short video

Practice Onboarding imports patient records from a prior EHR in nine guided steps. You review every row before it is saved; problem rows are held with a reason, and a results file shows what was imported.
