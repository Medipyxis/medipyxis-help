# What's new, in video

Short silent videos of recent changes in Medipyxis, each with a description of what is on screen and why it matters.

## How do signed and unsigned visits show on the Timeline and in the PDF?

[Signed visits on the Timeline](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/timeline-signoff/) · 1 short video

On a patient's Timeline, a locked visit names the clinician who signed it, and its PDF reads "Signed by" and "SIGNED & FINALIZED". An unsigned draft shows Draft and "Entered by" its author, and its PDF shows PENDING SIGNATURE with no signer.

## How does a patient import fill in a one-line address?

[One-line address on import](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/import-address-new/) · 1 short video

When a patient file lists the address on one line, the import fills in the street, city, state and ZIP on the new chart, and tidies the ZIP to 62999-0001.

## What happens when a re-import fills a missing address?

[Re-import fills a blank address](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/import-address-refill/) · 1 short video

Re-importing a file for a patient already on file with no address fills in that address, listed under Needs Follow-Up, and changes nothing else. A row whose address differs from the one on file is held for review, and the chart keeps its original address.

## How does a deceased patient’s chart describe the status?

[Deceased status on the chart](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/deceased-status-copy/) · 1 short video

A deceased patient’s chart now shows the Deceased status with the note “Recorded in error? Use Clear deceased under Appointment messages.”

## How are dates checked before a patient import?

[Dates on a patient import](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/preflight-dates/) · 1 short video

When you review a patient file before importing, dates written as MM/DD/YYYY are read month-first and shown converted (12/31/1955 becomes 1955-12-31, 04/05/1950 becomes 1950-04-05), and the review screen no longer says the file has mixed formats.

## How do I search the Fax Review Queue?

[Search the fax queue](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/fax-queue-search/) · 1 short video

You can search the Fax Review Queue by patient name or by a sender fax number, and you can widen the search to all fax text so a phrase in the subject is found too.

## How does Use last order fill the MRN?

[Use last order and the MRN](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/use-last-order-mrn/) · 1 short video

Use last order copies the patient’s previous order of that type into a new draft. If that earlier order’s ID / MRN was empty, the new form fills the chart MRN.

## How do I pick a wound from today's visit on an order?

[Today's wounds on an order](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/order-picker-todays-wounds/) · 1 short video

The wound dropdown on an order now includes wounds documented on today’s unsigned visit, labelled “(new from today’s visit),” as well as wounds already on the chart.

## How does a new order form get the chart MRN?

[Chart MRN on a new order](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/order-form-mrn-prefill/) · 1 short video

When you open a new Wound Care Supplies Order, the ID / MRN box is already filled with the chart MRN. The box stays editable.

## How do I upload my practice's consent form?

[Upload your consent form](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/consent-upload/) · 1 short video

In Administrator Hub, Consent Documentation, your practice can upload its own consent form as a PDF. Practices that have not uploaded a form see no change.

## Deceased card and filter

[Deceased card and filter](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/deceased-filter/) · 1 short video

All Patients has a Deceased card. Clicking it lists deceased patients, and the status filter shows Deceased.

## How do I record the indication on a debridement pop-up?

[Indication on debridement pop-ups](https://help.medipyxis.com/whats-new/debridement-indication/) · 1 short video

Indication is now the first field on the Sharp debridement and Enzymatic debridement pop-ups. The walkthrough shows the Indication list, the sharp choices Slough, Biofilm and Other with free text, and the enzymatic choices Necrotic / devitalized tissue and Eschar.
