# What's new, in video

Short silent videos of what's new in Medipyxis, each with a description of what's on screen, why it matters, and a text description of the video.

## How does Medipyxis handle rounding at a skilled nursing facility?

Medipyxis runs wound care rounding at skilled nursing and assisted living facilities from start to finish: set up the facility as a building, book every resident's visit for the day, see each resident, bill the visit with the facility on the claim, and send the facility its signed notes.

Watch the series (5 short videos): [Rounding at a nursing facility, start to finish](/whats-new/snf-rounding/)

## How do I move my practice's patient data into Medipyxis?

Practice Onboarding imports your patient records from your old EHR system, one spreadsheet (CSV file) at a time: patients first, then insurance, medications, allergies, conditions, emergency contacts, wounds and past visits. You see every row before it is saved, rows with problems are left out with a reason, and a results file tells you exactly what was imported.

Watch the video (1 short video): [Import your practice data](/whats-new/practice-data-import/)

## How do I use Medipyxis? Video training, from referral to signed visit

Eleven short videos walk through Medipyxis the way a wound care practice uses it: finding your way around, HR and staff onboarding, the referral CRM, adding a patient, assigning and booking them, documenting and signing the visit, orders and DME, Fleet Calendar, the Oversight Cockpit and Team Chat. Each video has on-screen captions and a full text transcript below it.

Watch the series (11 short videos): [How to use Medipyxis, module by module](/whats-new/medipyxis-training/)
