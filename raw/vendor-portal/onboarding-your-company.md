A clinic starts your company's onboarding by inviting you from its side. The invitation email links to a five-step onboarding wizard in the vendor portal (`/vendor/onboarding`). Each step is saved when you continue, and the same link brings you back to where you left off.

## Before you start

- You need the invitation email from the clinic. The link in it expires 30 days after the invitation was created.
- Have your company's name, legal name, EIN, NPI (if you have one), contact details, address, and a link to your signed Business Associate Agreement (BAA) ready.
- Steps 4 and 5 need you to be signed in to the vendor portal as an Admin.

If the clinic invites a company that is already registered in Medipyxis, the invitation is linked to that company instead of creating a second one.

## The five steps

| # | Step | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Profile** | Enter your company details and contact information. |
| 2 | **BAA** | Link your signed Business Associate Agreement. |
| 3 | **Modalities** | Pick the product categories you supply. |
| 4 | **Staff** | Invite your team. |
| 5 | **Catalog push** | Connect to the inviting clinic and propose your catalog to it. |

The header shows which step you are on, and a side panel shows your progress and the invitation details (who invited you, the email it was sent to, and when it expires). Use **Back** to return to an earlier step.

## Step 1 — Profile

Fill in **Company name** (required), **Legal name**, **EIN**, **NPI**, **Primary contact name**, **Phone**, **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP**, and **Website**, then continue. Your company is created with the status `pending_verification`, and your portal account is attached to it.

## Step 2 — BAA

Paste the link to your signed BAA in **BAA document URL** (required), then continue. The date you saved it is recorded as the signing date.

## Step 3 — Modalities

Tick every product category you supply. At least one is required. The options are:

- Amniotic / placental allografts
- Biologics — collagen & ECM
- PRP / autologous
- DME & offloading
- Negative pressure (NPWT)
- Compression & dressings
- Skin substitutes — synthetic
- Lab / diagnostics

## Step 4 — Staff

For each person, enter **Email**, **First name**, **Last name**, and **Role** (**Staff** or **Admin**). Click **+ Add another person** for more. Each person gets an email invitation to set their password.

## Step 5 — Catalog push

Click **Complete onboarding**. This approves your connection with the inviting clinic and proposes your catalog products to it. The clinic can accept or decline each product.

## After onboarding

- Add or remove portal users from **Staff** (Admins only).
- Push your catalog to other connected clinics from **Catalog → Push to clinic**. See [Manage your catalog](/vendor-portal/manage-catalog/).
