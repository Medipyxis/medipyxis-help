# Nurse daily cheat sheet

One page. Print it or keep it pinned. Everything you do in a typical day.

## Start of day

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| See today's appointments | **Fleet Calendar** (opens in Day view) |
| Preview the week | **Fleet Calendar** → **Week** view |
| Check your credentials | **Dashboard** → **My Credentials & Compliance** |

## During a visit

| Task | Where |
|---|---|
| Start the visit | **Fleet Calendar** → tap the appointment → **Start Visit** (or **Continue Visit** / **Resume Visit** for a note in progress) |
| Move between steps | Open the section you want from the wizard's list (Step 1 to Step 17, plus Step 2b) |
| Record a graft or product | **Interventions & Treatments** (Step 9) → pick the product and its lot |
| Record the box UID/UIN of a graft | **Procedure Supplies** (Step 13) → type it from the package |
| Fill billing codes | **Billing & Documentation** (Step 15) → **Pull Codes** (rule-based, no AI), then review every line |
| Sign & lock | Tick the attestation box and click **Complete Provider Attestation** (Step 17), then **Continue to Review** → **Sign & Lock** (then **Go to Billing**) |

## Saving your work

- The wizard **autosaves continuously** as you complete each field, so you rarely need to save by hand.
- The web app is online-only; there is no separate offline mode or manual sync.
- If you leave before **Sign & Lock**, the visit stays an unsigned draft. Drafts show on the Fleet Calendar and can be resumed.

## Shortcuts that save real time

| Shortcut | Action |
|---|---|
| `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` | Quick Jump — search pages, features or actions and go there |

## Who to ping when

| Problem | Channel |
|---|---|
| Scheduling conflict or clinical question | Message your manager or team in **Team Chat** (New Conversation or New Group Chat) |
| Question about one patient | The **Team Chat** tab on that patient's chart |
| Credential about to expire | **Dashboard** → **My Credentials & Compliance** |

## End of day

1. All visits signed? Check the Fleet Calendar for visits still in draft.
2. Mileage & Expenses is not switched on yet: its page shows a Coming Soon banner. Do not expect a working mileage log.
