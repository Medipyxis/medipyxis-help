# How do I pick a wound from today's visit on an order?

The wound dropdown on an order now includes wounds documented on today’s unsigned visit, labelled “(new from today’s visit),” as well as wounds already on the chart.

The video has no sound, and a text description is below it. It was recorded with a made-up test patient; staff names and emails are blurred. It plays at half speed so the fields are easier to read.

## What changed in the order wound picker?

*Available now · 1 minute 19 seconds (half speed)*

Video (no sound, 1:19): [order-picker-todays-wounds.mp4](https://help.medipyxis.com/assets/videos/whats-new/order-picker-todays-wounds.mp4)

Picking a chart wound fills its location, type, and measured size from the chart. Picking a wound labelled “Right Heel (new from today’s visit)” fills Location with Right Heel. The other measurement fields stay empty, with the helper text “No measurement recorded for this wound,” because the draft visit had no measurement yet. The form is closed without saving.

**Why it matters:** You can order supplies for a wound you just documented, without waiting for the visit to be signed.

**Text description of this video**

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (1 minute 19 seconds).

A clinician starts a Wound Care Supplies Order, chooses a vendor, and opens the wound dropdown. They pick a chart wound (Wound #1, Left Heel). The form fills the wound number, location, type, and measured size (1 × 1 × 0.1 cm) from the chart.

They then pick “Right Heel (new from today’s visit).” Location is filled with Right Heel. Wound number, type, and measurements stay empty, and the form shows “No measurement recorded for this wound.” The form is closed without saving.
