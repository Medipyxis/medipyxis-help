# How do I search the Fax Review Queue?

You can search the Fax Review Queue by patient name or by a sender fax number, and you can widen the search to all fax text so a phrase in the subject is found too.

The video has no sound, and a text description is below it. It was recorded with a made-up test patient; staff names and emails are blurred. It plays at half speed so the fields are easier to read.

## What changed in the Fax Review Queue?

*Available now · 2 minutes 2 seconds (half speed)*

Video (no sound, 2:02): [fax-queue-search.mp4](https://help.medipyxis.com/assets/videos/whats-new/fax-queue-search.mp4)

A patient-name search narrows the queue to matching faxes. A sender fax-number search finds faxes from that sender. With Search all fax text turned off, a phrase that appears only in the subject finds nothing (“No faxes found”). Turning the switch on finds those faxes and marks each one “Matched: Subject.”

**Why it matters:** You can find a fax without scrolling the whole queue, including when the match is in the subject rather than the patient name.

**Text description of this video**

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (2 minutes 2 seconds).

A clinician is in the Fax Review Queue and types a patient name. The queue narrows to the fax for that patient. They then search with a sender fax number, and two faxes from that sender are listed.

They search a phrase that is not in the patient name, with Search all fax text turned off, and the queue shows “No faxes found.” They turn Search all fax text on. The same phrase matches two faxes, and each row is marked “Matched: Subject.”
