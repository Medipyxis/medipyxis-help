# How do I record the indication on a debridement pop-up?

On the Sharp debridement and Enzymatic debridement pop-ups, Indication is now the first field. You pick the reason for the procedure before you fill in the rest of the pop-up.

The video has no sound, and a text description is below it. It was recorded with a made-up test patient; staff names and emails are blurred. It plays at half speed so the fields are easier to read.

## What changed on the debridement pop-ups?

*Available now · 4 minutes 21 seconds (half speed)*

Video (no sound, 4:21): [debridement-indication.mp4](https://help.medipyxis.com/assets/videos/whats-new/debridement-indication.mp4)

Indication is the first field on both pop-ups. For sharp debridement the walkthrough selects Slough, Biofilm, and Other, and types “Fibrinous wound bed” in the Other box. For enzymatic debridement it selects Necrotic / devitalized tissue and Eschar. Both interventions are saved on the visit, which is left unsigned.

**Why it matters:** The reason for each debridement is recorded first, in the same place, whether the intervention is sharp or enzymatic.

**Text description of this video**

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (4 minutes 21 seconds).

A clinician is in a visit and opens the Sharp debridement pop-up. Indication is the first field. The Indication choices shown are Necrotic / devitalized tissue, Slough, Eschar, Biofilm, Callus / hyperkeratosis, Signs of infection, Foreign material / debris, and Other. The clinician selects Slough, Biofilm, and Other, and types “Fibrinous wound bed” in the Other free-text box, then saves the sharp debridement.

They then open the Enzymatic debridement pop-up, where Indication is again the first field, with the same list of choices. They select Necrotic / devitalized tissue and Eschar, then save. Both interventions appear on the visit. The visit is left unsigned.
