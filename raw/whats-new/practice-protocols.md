# How do Practice Protocols work?

Practice Protocols let your practice write its own wound-care steps, or adopt one of the optional examples, and show them to clinicians during visits. Your practice's administrators and medical directors choose, write and turn on these protocols. Medipyxis never turns one on. A protocol is guidance only: it doesn't block saving, signing or finishing a visit.

The videos have no sound, and a text description is below each one. They were recorded with made-up test data; staff names and emails are blurred. They play at half speed so the fields are easier to read.

## Questions and answers about Practice Protocols

### How does a practice set up a protocol?

An administrator or medical director at that facility opens Administrator, then Practice Protocols, and picks a wound condition. They can start from an optional example (Diabetic Foot Ulcer, Venous Leg Ulcer, Arterial Ulcer, Pressure Injury, Surgical Wound, Trauma Wound and Burn have one) or write their own steps. Edits save to a draft that reaches no clinician. To turn it on, they click Review and adopt, enter their name and title, accept the declaration and click Adopt and turn on.

### Do protocols block signing or finishing a visit?

No. The guide in the visit only reads the visit; it saves nothing, and no save depends on it. If the guide fails to load, the visit stays usable. Items marked Not met or Unknown don't stop Continue to Review. After a visit is signed, the wound's protocol result is recorded separately, and that step can't affect the signature.

### Can Medipyxis turn a protocol on for us?

No. Only your practice's administrators and medical directors at that facility can edit, adopt or turn off a protocol. Medipyxis staff can view protocols but can't edit or adopt them. Examples are unverified starting points and reach no clinician unless your practice copies, reviews and adopts them.

### How do protocols affect charting?

Once a protocol is adopted for a wound condition, the visit shows a Clinic protocol guide for wounds with that condition. It shows the week of care, the area change, and each standard-of-care item as Met, Not met or Unknown, with Go to links to the matching sections. An item shows Met or Not met only when the visit has a field that answers it; otherwise it shows Unknown. The Wound Cockpit shows a protocol badge, and the Clinician guide lists every adopted protocol.

### Can protocols reduce charting?

Not today. Protocols guide only. They don't fill in the note, place orders or change signing. Go to links take the clinician to the right section faster, but the clinician still documents everything as before.

### Can we change or turn off a protocol?

Yes. Editing an adopted protocol starts a new draft while the current version stays on, until you adopt the new version. Turn off removes the protocol from the visit and the Clinician guide right away, and past versions are kept.

### Is this available now?

Yes. Practice Protocols is in the current release. As of 11/28/2026, no practice on the live site has adopted a protocol yet, so clinicians won't see a guide until their practice adopts one.
