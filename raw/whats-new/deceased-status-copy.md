## What changed on a deceased patient’s chart?

*Available now · 24 seconds (half speed)*

Video (no sound, 24 seconds): [deceased-status-copy.mp4](https://help.medipyxis.com/assets/videos/whats-new/deceased-status-copy.mp4)

A deceased patient’s chart now shows the Deceased status with the note “Recorded in error? Use Clear deceased under Appointment messages.”

**Why it matters:** The chart no longer says the status can be changed, and it points to the one way to undo a deceased record entered by mistake.

**Text description of this video**

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (24 seconds).

A clinician is on a deceased patient’s chart. Patient Status shows a Deceased badge. Next to it is the note “Recorded in error? Use Clear deceased under Appointment messages.” Appointment messages are off, and a Clear deceased action is available. The chart does not say the status can be changed.
