# February 2026 release notes

Changes merged between 2026-02-01 and 2026-02-28. This is not a complete list: it covers selected user-facing changes that are still in the current release.

---

- **Education — Nine patient and caregiver education bundles** were added, with a preview. (PR #1368, #1376)
- **Wound history — Future dates are blocked in Historic Measurements.** (PR #1456)
- **ZusHealth — Pre-charting data from ZusHealth.** A pre-charting service reads a patient's external record from ZusHealth. (PR #1345)

Later changes are in the [March 2026](/release-notes/2026-03/) and [April 2026](/release-notes/2026-04/) release notes.
