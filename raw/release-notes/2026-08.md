Changes merged between 2026-08-01 and 2026-09-01. Items are grouped by module. This is not a complete list: it covers selected user-facing changes that are still in the current release.

---

## Provider Attestation & Notes

- ★ **APRN is its own credential.** APRN and NP are separate options in the attestation dropdown. See [APRN as a signature credential](/modules/visit-wizard-ehr/aprn-credential/). (PR #3369)
- ★ **Signature timestamp is readable and shows its time zone.** See [How the signature timestamp prints on a signed note](/modules/visit-wizard-ehr/signature-timestamp/). (PR #3363)
- ★ **Provider credential prints on the signature line** beside the provider's name. (PR #3358)
- ★ **No made-up post-debridement measurement.** The note no longer prints a post-debridement measurement that was never taken. See [Note-honesty guardrails](/modules/visit-wizard-ehr/note-honesty-guardrails/). (PR #3334)

---

## Billing & Claims

- ★ **Claims no longer bill $0.00.** The resolved charge is carried onto each claim line, and a $0.00 claim is not sent. See [Charge Master and the $0.00 claim guard](/modules/billing/charge-master-and-zero-dollar-guard/). (PR #3347)
- ★ **Retired CPT 99343 is no longer suggested.** See [Retired CPT guard](/modules/billing/retired-cpt-guard/). (PR #3326)
- **Diagnosis codes are checked against the billable code list.** (PR #3323)
- **Coding Review drawer opens on the claim it codes.** (PR #3376)
- **PTAN field in payer enrollment.** See [PTAN and payer enrollment](/modules/billing/ptan-and-payer-enrollment/). (PR #3238)
