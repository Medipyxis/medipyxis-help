# Use the referral detail panel

Clicking any kanban card opens the detail panel on the right. It has six tabs.

## Tabs

- **Details** — the referral and patient details captured at intake.
- **Documents** — files attached to the referral.
- **Chat** — a conversation about this referral. It is a Team Chat conversation, so it also appears in Team Chat.
- **Precharting** — preparation for the first visit.
- **History** — a history of activity on the referral.
- **Notes** — notes on the referral.

## Common actions

- **Move a card** — drag it between columns on the board.
- **Assign a clinician** — use **Assign** on the card. After a card is moved to **Assigned**, you are prompted to select a provider to complete assignment.
- **Non-Admit** — drag the card into the **Non-Admit** column.
- **Face Sheet** — the panel can generate a **Face Sheet** for the patient.
