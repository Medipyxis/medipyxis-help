# Manage your day as a clinician

See the visits assigned to you for the day and launch each one from the Fleet Calendar.

## Before you start

- You are logged in as yourself.
- Appointments have been scheduled for you on the Fleet Calendar. If nothing appears for today, contact your scheduler.

## Steps

### See your visits

1. **Open the Fleet Calendar** and use the **All Providers** filter to show only your own column. You can also open **My Calendar** with the **Calendar** button on the Patient Management page; it lists only your appointments, but it has no Start Visit button.
2. **Review the day.** Each appointment card shows the patient, the time, and the status. Click a card to open **Appointment Details**, which shows the purpose, notes and route notes the scheduler added.

### Start a visit

1. **Click the appointment** on the Fleet Calendar to open **Appointment Details**.
2. **Click Start Visit.** The [Visit Wizard](/modules/visit-wizard-ehr/overview/) opens for that patient and appointment, so you do not have to search for the patient.
3. **Document and sign.** Work the visit end to end and finish with **Sign & Lock** — see [Sign Off](/modules/visit-wizard-ehr/sign-off/).

## Result

As you work, each appointment's status updates on the calendar — **Progress Note In Progress** once you open the wizard and start editing, then **Visit Completed** once the note is signed — so your scheduler can follow progress throughout the day.

Resuming a draft? Click the same appointment. The button reads **Continue Visit** or **Resume Visit** and takes you back to the visit in progress.
