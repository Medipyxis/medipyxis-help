# Team Chat overview

Team Chat is where your team messages each other inside Medipyxis: direct messages and group chats with the people at your facility. Open it from **Team Chat** in the sidebar.

![Team Chat main view showing Conversations and Team tabs](../../assets/team-chat/main.png)

_Team Chat — conversation list on the left, thread view on the right._

## Two tabs

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Conversations** | Your direct and group conversations. A red dot on the tab means one of them has unread messages. On small screens the tab is labelled **Chats**. |
| **Team** | The people at your facility. Search, filter by role and by Online or Offline, and sort A-Z. Click a person to start a direct chat. |

Click a conversation in the list to open the thread on the right. A conversation linked to a visit, a claim or a referral shows a **Visit**, **Billing** or **Referral** badge. The discussion of an HR announcement shows a **Kudos** badge.

## Sending a message

1. **Click** the conversation you want to post in.
2. **Type your message** in the box at the bottom of the thread. You can add emoji with the emoji button.
3. **To flag it as urgent,** tick **Mark as urgent** before you send.
4. **Press Enter** (or click the send button) to post. Shift+Enter starts a new line.

The other people in the conversation get an in-app notification ("New message from …", or "Urgent message from …") with an **Open Chat** link.

Team Chat messages are text and emoji. You cannot attach files or @mention people.

## Starting a new conversation

1. **Click New Conversation** (one person) or **New Group Chat** (several people) at the top of Team Chat. You can also click a person on the Team tab.
2. **Enter a title and pick the people.**
3. **Click Create Conversation,** then type your first message.

The menu on a conversation has **Clear Chat** and **Delete Conversation**.

## Patient conversations

The **Chat** tab in a Referral Intake card is a conversation about that referral. It is a Team Chat conversation, so it also appears in Team Chat.

A patient's chart has its own **Team Chat** tab for conversations about that patient. It reminds you that "all messages are part of the patient record".

Keep patient names, dates of birth and other protected health information out of general Team Chat conversations. Discuss a patient in that patient's chart or referral chat.
