Post and manage announcements

how-to ⏱ 5 min read Admin Clinician Business development biller Intake Updated 2026-11-11

Announcements send a message to your team. Everyone reads them on the Company Announcements page, which the View Announcements & Kudos button on the Dashboard opens. Use them for compliance notices, HR updates, culture shout-outs, and operational reminders for a chosen set of roles.

Before you start

  • Creating, editing and deleting announcements on the HR & Compliance Announcements page needs an administrator role.
  • Anyone who can open Company Announcements can read announcements, join the discussion, and send Kudos.

Categories

Pick the category that matches your intent. The reader page shows each one with its own colored badge.

Category Use for
Compliance Policy changes, mandatory acknowledgements, regulatory updates
HR Benefits, PTO, onboarding, org changes
Culture Team celebrations, values reminders, morale
Events All-hands, offsites, training sessions
Operations Workflow changes, downtime notices, process updates
Kudos Peer recognition

Compose an announcement

  1. Open the composer. In HR & Compliance, click Announcements, then New Announcement.
  2. Write the Title (required) and pick the Category.
  3. Write the Message Body. Markdown is supported.
  4. Pick the audience under Target Audience: tick Send to All Team Members, or use Select Roles to choose one or more roles. The composer shows how many users each role would reach.
  5. Attach files if needed (PDF, JPG, PNG, DOC or DOCX, up to 10 MB each).
  6. Pick delivery timing under Delivery Settings: Send Immediately, or Schedule for Later with a date and time in your local time zone. In this release a scheduled announcement is saved as Scheduled but is not released at that time, so it does not appear on Company Announcements. Use Send Immediately for anything your team must see.
  7. Save.

Kudos

Kudos are short peer-recognition posts. On Company Announcements, click Send Kudos, write a title and message, and send. You do not need an administrator role. Kudos always go to all team members and are sent immediately.

Reader page

  • Announcements are listed newest first, each with its category badge.
  • Each announcement can have a Discussion thread. The thread is a Team Chat conversation for that announcement, so replies stay with the announcement instead of the main chat.

Edit or retract

Administrators edit and delete announcements on the HR & Compliance Announcements page:

  1. Find the announcement and click Edit. The composer opens with the current content; update it and save.
  2. To retract, click Delete and confirm. The announcement is removed permanently.

Troubleshooting

Symptom Likely cause What to do
Edit, Delete or New Announcement is greyed out You are not an administrator Ask an administrator to post or change the announcement
“Please select roles or choose Send to All Team Members” No audience picked Tick Send to All Team Members or choose at least one role
Scheduled announcement never appeared Scheduled announcements are not released automatically yet Post it again with Send Immediately
Attachment won't upload File over 10 MB Split or compress the file and retry
