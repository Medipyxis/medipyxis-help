Walk through the Add New Patient form

how-to ⏱ 6 min read Intake Updated 2026-11-10

There is no separate Intake Wizard, no Start Intake button, and no Publish Patient Record step. Adding a patient is the Add New Patient form, opened with Add New Referral from Referral Intake. This page walks through that form in the order the sections appear.

For the short version, see Add a new patient from Referral Intake. For inbound faxes, see Review the Fax Review Queue.

Open the form

  1. Click Referral Intake in the sidebar (the board heading is Patient Intake).
  2. Click Add New Referral.
  3. The form heading is Add New Patient.

Anyone whose role can open Referral Intake can use this form. The board columns you will return to are New Referrals, Info Needed, Financial, Ready for Assignment, Assigned, and Non-Admit. There is no New or Triaged column.

Care Type

Choose Wound Care (“Wound assessment & treatment”) or Primary Care (“General health & preventive care”). This is the first section on the form, not a last step after the patient already exists.

Patient Demographics and address

Required: First Name, Last Name, Date of Birth. Optional: Middle Initial, Sex (Male, Female, Other), Marital Status, Employment Status, Primary Phone Number, Secondary Phone Number, Email Address, Street Address, Lives at, City, State, ZIP Code.

Phone and address may be left blank. If you do enter a phone number, use (xxx) xxx-xxxx.

Referring practice and PCP

Search Referral Source for a practice already in your CRM, or click Add New Practice. Complete referring individual, dates, referral ID, phone, fax, physician, NPI, and Assigned BD Rep as you have them. Then fill in Patient's Primary Care Physician the same way if you have a PCP.

Insurance

Click Add Insurance. Enter Insurance Provider, Policy Number / Member Number, optional Group Number, subscriber name, Payer ID, and Subscriber Relationship. Click Verify to run eligibility through the payer connection. Add another row for secondary coverage. Results appear in Insurance Verification Summary.

Urgency and billing

Enter a Referral Reason and set Urgency Level to Routine, Urgent, or Emergent. Optional billing checkboxes cover employment, auto accident, and other accident, plus Accept Assignment of Benefits.

The form does not include a wound body map, etiology, ICD-10 picker, allergies, or history section. Capture those at the first visit.

Create the patient

Optionally check Send digital consent form via SMS upon approval, or click Send Consent SMS if a phone number is on the form. Then click Create Patient (or Save Offline if you are offline). Cancel discards the form.

The patient is created immediately. A success message includes the MRN when one is assigned, and you return to the Patient Intake board with the card in New Referrals. Nothing named Save Draft or Publish Patient Record appears on this form, and the card is not moved to Assigned by creating the patient.
