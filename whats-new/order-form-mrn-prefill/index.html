How does a new order form get the chart MRN?

whats-new Orders Updated 2026-11-25

When you open a new Wound Care Supplies Order, the ID / MRN box is already filled with the chart MRN. The box stays editable.

What is prefilled on a new order form?

No sound · 1 minute 13 seconds (half speed) · a text description of the video is below

The new form shows the patient name and date of birth, and ID / MRN already filled from the chart. Phone and address stay empty when the chart has no values there. The clinician clicks into the ID / MRN box and edits it, then closes the form without saving.

Why it matters: The chart MRN is on the form from the start, and you can still change it before you send.

Text description of this video

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (1 minute 13 seconds).

A clinician starts a new Wound Care Supplies Order and chooses a vendor. They scroll to Patient Information. Patient name and date of birth are filled. ID / MRN is already filled with the chart MRN. Phone and address are empty.

They click into the ID / MRN box and edit the value, showing that the field stays editable. The form is closed without saving.
