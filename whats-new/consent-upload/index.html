How do I upload my practice's consent form?

whats-new Administrator Updated 2026-11-26

In Administrator Hub, Consent Documentation, your practice can upload its own consent form as a PDF. Your own lawyer reviews the form, and Medipyxis stores the file and runs the signature workflow. Practices that have not uploaded a form see no change.

The video has no sound, and a text description is below it. It was recorded with a made-up test practice; the staff name and the upload date are blurred. It plays at half speed so the screens are easier to read.

How do I upload my practice's consent form?

No sound · 1 minute 9 seconds (half speed) · a text description of the video is below

An administrator opens Administrator Hub and then Consent Documentation. The page shows No consent form uploaded and says the practice provides the consent form and its own lawyer reviews it. They choose a PDF, tick the box confirming their own lawyer reviewed the file, and click Upload form. The page then shows Version 1 with the file name, a View uploaded form link, and a Replace with a PDF section. The video does not open the PDF and does not show the patient signing screen.

Why it matters: Your consent paperwork can be your own. Practices that have not uploaded a form see no change: the page says patients can still use the current form until one is uploaded.
