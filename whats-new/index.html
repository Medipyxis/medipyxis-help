What's new, in video

whats-new Updated 2026-11-26

Short silent videos of recent changes in Medipyxis, each with a description of what is on screen and why it matters.

How does a deceased patient's chart describe the status?

Deceased status on the chart · 1 short video A deceased patient’s chart now shows the Deceased status with the note “Recorded in error? Use Clear deceased under Appointment messages.”

How are dates checked before a patient import?

Dates on a patient import · 1 short video When you review a patient file before importing, dates written as MM/DD/YYYY are read month-first and shown converted, and the review screen no longer says the file has mixed formats.

How do I search the Fax Review Queue?

Search the fax queue · 1 short video You can search the Fax Review Queue by patient name or by a sender fax number, and you can widen the search to all fax text so a phrase in the subject is found too.

How does Use last order fill the MRN?

Use last order and the MRN · 1 short video Use last order copies the patient’s previous order of that type into a new draft. If that earlier order’s ID / MRN was empty, the new form fills the chart MRN.

How do I pick a wound from today's visit on an order?

Today's wounds on an order · 1 short video The wound dropdown on an order now includes wounds documented on today’s unsigned visit, labelled “(new from today’s visit),” as well as wounds already on the chart.

How does a new order form get the chart MRN?

Chart MRN on a new order · 1 short video When you open a new Wound Care Supplies Order, the ID / MRN box is already filled with the chart MRN. The box stays editable.

How do I upload my practice's consent form?

Upload your consent form · 1 short video In Administrator Hub, Consent Documentation, your practice can upload its own consent form as a PDF. Practices that have not uploaded a form see no change.

Deceased card and filter

Deceased card and filter · 1 short video All Patients has a Deceased card. Clicking it lists deceased patients, and the status filter shows Deceased.

How do I record the indication on a debridement pop-up?

Indication on debridement pop-ups · 1 short video Indication is now the first field on the Sharp debridement and Enzymatic debridement pop-ups. The walkthrough shows the Indication list, the sharp choices Slough, Biofilm and Other with free text, and the enzymatic choices Necrotic / devitalized tissue and Eschar.
