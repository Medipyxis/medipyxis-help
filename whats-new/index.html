Available now

Available now CRM

Nursing homes and assisted living facilities as buildings in CRM

What you're seeing

  • Adding a building in CRM with the practice type Skilled Nursing Facility.
  • Opening the building. Skilled nursing and assisted living buildings get three extra tabs.
  • Providers: who rounds at the building and on which days, plus anyone who backs them up or covers for set dates.
  • Residents: the patients whose chart says they live there now, with the date of their last signed visit there.
  • Clinical Notes: signed visits that took place at the building, for a date range you pick.

Why it matters

Everything about a building is on one page: who covers it, who lives there, and which notes have been signed there. You no longer have to piece this together from the schedule and individual charts.

Available now Fleet Calendar

Book a whole facility day at once

What you're seeing

  • In Fleet Calendar, Book facility day: pick the building, date, provider, start time and minutes per resident.
  • The building's residents are listed. Tick the ones to see and pick a visit type for each.
  • Each ticked resident gets a visit back to back (9:00, 9:20, 9:40, 10:00). A resident who is already booked that day is left alone.

Why it matters

A morning of rounds takes one booking instead of one booking per resident, and every visit records the building it happens at.

Available now Billing and CRM

Claims name the building, and the building gets its notes

What you're seeing

  • A claim from a nursing facility visit. Place of service 31 came from the booking.
  • The Service Facility card, Box 32 on the claim form, names the building where the care was given and its address.
  • Back on the building's Clinical Notes tab, Download notes (.zip) collects the signed notes for the dates you pick.
  • The download holds each clinical note without its billing section, any amendments as separate files, and an index.

Why it matters

The claim says where the care happened, not just where your office is. When a facility asks for its residents' notes, you can send them in one download with no billing detail.

Available now Visit Wizard billing

Pull Codes suggests the visit code for where the visit happened

Video to be recorded on the demo system (demo data only) before this entry is published.

What you're seeing

  • For a visit at a nursing facility (place of service 31 or 32), Pull Codes suggests the visit code from the nursing facility code family.
  • For a visit at the patient's home or residence (place of service 12, 13, 14, 33 or 55), it suggests the code from the home visit family.
  • Office visits, and visits with no place of service, get the same office code as before.

Why it matters

The suggested visit code matches the setting, so the biller has less to correct by hand. The suggestion is a starting point: you still review every code before signing.

Next update Patients

Quick Add keeps the insurance you type

Video to be recorded on the demo system (demo data only) before this entry is published.

What you're seeing

  • Adding a new patient with Quick Add and typing in their insurance.
  • The patient's chart then shows that plan as their primary insurance, marked as not yet verified.
  • If the plan can't be saved, the patient is still saved and a message tells you to add the plan on the chart.

Why it matters

Before this update, the insurance typed in Quick Add was not kept, so the claim for that visit had no payer. Now the plan follows the patient from the first visit.

Next update Visit Wizard billing

Pulled codes stay on the wound cards

Video to be recorded on the demo system (demo data only) before this entry is published.

What you're seeing

  • Clicking Pull Codes and seeing the codes fill the wound cards.
  • The codes stay there. The button then reads Re-pull Codes.
  • If you change the wounds after pulling, a banner tells you the codes may be out of date.

Why it matters

Some visits showed "Pulled 3 codes" while the cards stayed empty, because a check running in the background cleared them a few seconds later. Now the codes you pull are the codes you review and sign.

Next update Notifications

Alerts arrive by email as well as in the bell

Video to be recorded on the demo system (demo data only) before this entry is published.

What you're seeing

  • An alert in the bell at the top of the app, and the same alert arriving by email.
  • Settings → Notifications, where Pause Alerts keeps alerts in the bell only.
  • Text messages show as Coming soon.

Why it matters

You hear about things that need you even when Medipyxis isn't open. If you chose to pause alerts, that choice is kept. Team chat messages stay in the app and are not emailed.
