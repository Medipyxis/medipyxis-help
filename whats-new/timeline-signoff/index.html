How do signed and unsigned visits show on the Timeline and in the PDF?

whats-new Patients Updated 2026-11-27

On a patient's Timeline, a locked visit names the clinician who signed it, and its PDF reads "Signed by" and "SIGNED & FINALIZED". An unsigned draft shows Draft and "Entered by" its author, and its PDF shows PENDING SIGNATURE with no signer.

What changed on signed and unsigned visits?

No sound · 121 seconds (half speed) · a text description of the video is below

On a patient's Timeline, a locked visit names the clinician who signed it, and its PDF reads "Signed by" and "SIGNED & FINALIZED". An unsigned draft shows Draft and "Entered by" its author, and its PDF shows PENDING SIGNATURE with no signer.

Why it matters: You can tell at a glance which visits are signed and by whom, and an unsigned draft never looks signed.

Text description of this video

The video has no sound and plays at half speed (121 seconds).

On a patient's Timeline, a locked visit names the clinician who signed it and shows Status Finalized. Its PDF reads "Signed by" and "SIGNED & FINALIZED". An unsigned draft on the same Timeline shows Draft and "Entered by" its author. Its PDF shows blank signature lines and PENDING SIGNATURE, with no signer.
