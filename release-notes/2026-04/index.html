April 2026 release notes

release-note Clinician Intake Admin Billing Business development Vendor Updated 2026-11-11

Changes merged between 2026-04-01 and 2026-04-24. Items are grouped by module. This is not a complete list: it covers selected user-facing changes that are still in the current release.

Fleet Calendar

  • Fleet Calendar — Week view and status legend. A week view was added alongside the day view, with a color legend for appointment status. (PR #1587)
  • Fleet Calendar — Delete an appointment. (PR #1592)

Oversight Cockpit

  • Oversight Cockpit — Heat Map. (PR #1646)
  • Oversight Cockpit — Operations KPIs dashboard. (PR #1662)
  • Oversight Cockpit — Referral Health dashboard shows real data. (PR #1664)
  • Oversight Cockpit — Back to Analytics button on the dashboards. (PR #1682)

Business Development

  • Business Development — Next Best Action queue suggests which practice to contact next. (PR #1654)

Dashboard

  • Industry News — news feeds with video and media embeds, without leaving Medipyxis. (PR #1644)

Visit Wizard / EHR

  • LCD Navigator rebuilt and shown to nurses in the Visit Wizard. (PR #1612, #1625)
  • IVR Readiness window. (PR #1614)
  • Patient Allergies card, with allergies synced to DoseSpot. (PR #1689)
  • Send via eRx opens DoseSpot on the right patient. (PR #1686)

Inventory

  • Inventory — Providers can write off stock (damaged, lost, expired, or other), and a My Inventory link was added for providers. (PR #1636)

Later changes are in the May 2026 release notes.
