August 2026 release notes

release-note Clinician Intake Admin Billing Business development Vendor Updated 2026-11-11

Changes merged between 2026-08-01 and 2026-09-01. Items are grouped by module. This is not a complete list: it covers selected user-facing changes that are still in the current release.

Provider Attestation & Notes

  • ★ APRN is its own credential. APRN and NP are separate options in the attestation dropdown. See APRN as a signature credential. (PR #3369)
  • ★ Signature timestamp is readable and shows its time zone. See How the signature timestamp prints on a signed note. (PR #3363)
  • ★ Provider credential prints on the signature line beside the provider's name. (PR #3358)
  • ★ No made-up post-debridement measurement. The note no longer prints a post-debridement measurement that was never taken. See Note-honesty guardrails. (PR #3334)

Billing & Claims

  • ★ Claims no longer bill $0.00. The resolved charge is carried onto each claim line, and a $0.00 claim is not sent. See Charge Master and the $0.00 claim guard. (PR #3347)
  • ★ Retired CPT 99343 is no longer suggested. See Retired CPT guard. (PR #3326)
  • Diagnosis codes are checked against the billable code list. (PR #3323)
  • Coding Review drawer opens on the claim it codes. (PR #3376)
  • PTAN field in payer enrollment. See PTAN and payer enrollment. (PR #3238)

ZusHealth

  • One pull button in pre-charting, with a summary of what was imported. (PR #3224)

Notifications

  • ★ Email notifications launched; SMS and push are "coming soon". (PR #3330) Update (Nov 2026): SMS launched on October 30 (PR #3931). Push is still "coming soon".

Consent

  • ★ Typed-name signature as a backup way to sign a consent. (PR #3253)

Home Health

  • "Add New Agency" in the home health picker. (PR #3353)
