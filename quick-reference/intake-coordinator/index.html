Intake coordinator cheat sheet

reference ⏱ 2 min read Intake Updated 2026-11-10

Your daily loop: add or approve new patients, finish missing information on the Patient Intake board, then assign a clinician and schedule the first visit from Fleet Calendar.

Two ways to add a patient

If the referral arrived…Do this
By phone, email, or paperOpen Referral Intake → Add New Referral → complete the Add New Patient form → Create Patient. Only First Name, Last Name, and Date of Birth are required.
By faxOpen Fax Review Queue (sidebar, or the Fax Queue button on the board) → open the row → Approve & Create Patient. Or Save Changes / Reject as needed.

Either way, the new card appears in the New Referrals column. The patient record already exists at that point.

Work the board

  • Drag cards through Info Needed, Financial, Ready for Assignment, and Assigned as the work proceeds. Use Assign on the card to pick a clinician.
  • Schedule the first visit from Fleet Calendar (also a button on the board header).
  • If the patient will not be admitted, drag the card to Non-Admit.
  • Press ⌘K (Windows: Ctrl+K) and type to jump to Referral Intake, New Referrals (the Add New Patient form), or Fax Review Queue.

Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeFix
Create Patient does not completeFirst Name, Last Name, or Date of Birth is missingFill those three fields.
A phone number is rejectedFormat is not (xxx) xxx-xxxxCorrect the format, or leave the field blank.
You cannot see Referral IntakeThe role does not have the Referral Intake featureAsk an administrator to review Organization Role Config. The intake-oriented role is Referral Coordinator.
